# Pause or unpause a facet definition
{: .no_toc }

When a facet definition is paused, all facet instances associated with the facet definition are hidden on the interface. They cannot be updated or modified whilst paused.

## Pause a facet definition

To pause a facet definition, open the `Definitions` navigation menu and click `Facet Definitions`.

Locate the facet definition to be paused.

Click the `Pause` icon.

The facet definition, and all of the facet instances are paused.

## Unpause a facet definition

To unpause a facet definition, open the `Definitions` navigation menu and click `Facet Definitions`.

Locate the facet definition to be enabled.

Click the `Play` icon.

The facet definition, and all of the facet instances are made active again.
