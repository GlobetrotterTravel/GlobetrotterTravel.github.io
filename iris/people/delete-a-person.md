# Delete a person
{: .no_toc }

When a person is deleted, all facet instances and organization memberships associated with the person are also deleted. For this reason, deleting persons should be considered carefully.

## Delete a person

To delete a person, open the `Resources` navigation menu and click `People`.

Locate the person to be deleted.

Click the `Trash Can` icon. A confirmation dialog appears.

Click `Delete`.

The person, and all facet instances and organization memberships associated with the person are deleted.
