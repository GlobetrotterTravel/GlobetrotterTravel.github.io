# Pause or unpause a field definition
{: .no_toc }

When a field definition is paused, all field instances associated with the field definition are also paused.

## Pause a field definition

To pause a field definition, open the `Definitions` navigation menu and click `Field Definitions`.

Locate the field definition to be paused.

Click the `Pause` icon.

The field definition, and all of the field instances are paused.

## Unpause a field definition

To unpause a field definition, open the `Definitions` navigation menu and click `Field Definitions`.

Locate the field definition to be enabled.

Click the `Play` icon.

The field definition, and all of the field instances are made active again.
