# Delete a secret
{: .no_toc }

To delete a secret, navigate to the `System` navigation menu and click `Secrets`.

Locate the secret you want to delete and click the `Trash Can` icon.

A confirmation dialog appears. Click `Delete`. The secret is deleted.
