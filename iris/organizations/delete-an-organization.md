# Delete an organization
{: .no_toc }

When an organization is deleted, all resources associated with the organization are also deleted. For this reason, deleting organizations should be considered carefully.

## Delete an organization

To delete a organization, open the `Resources` navigation menu and click `Organizations`.

Locate the organization to be deleted.

Click the `Trash Can` icon. A confirmation dialog appears.

Click `Delete`.

The organization, and all of the resources (fields, hybrid lists, entitlements and people) associated with the organization are deleted.
